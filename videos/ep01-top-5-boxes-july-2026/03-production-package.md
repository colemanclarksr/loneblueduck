## 9) Motion Graphics Instructions

**Brand system:** dark navy (#0B1220) base, neon green (#22C55E) for gains/BUY, red (#EF4444) for AVOID, gold (#EAB308) for grades/trophies. Font: heavy condensed sans (e.g., Archivo Black / Anton) for stamps, Inter for data labels. Every stat pinned to a corner "source chip" (small gray tag: "DACW 7/24/26", "eBay solds 7/24/26").

1. **Price odometer:** rolling-digit morph old→new price, 0.8s, ease-out, chime at settle. Used for every price reveal.
2. **Scorecard template:** right-third panel, slides in over 0.4s: Investment Score (big), then Rarity/Liquidity/Upside/Downside/Risk/LT Hold/ST Flip as 7 mini-bars filling sequentially (0.1s stagger), Grade stamps last with impact SFX.
3. **Buy Board recap table:** 9 rows, each reveals with a 0.25s slide+fade; BUY rows tint green, WAIT row tints red and shakes 2px.
4. **Chart standard:** all line charts draw L→R over 1.2s, endpoint pulse, delta badge (+90%) stamps at 80% draw.
5. **Prospect ladder:** vertical rank ladder; top two icons float up and off ("GRADUATED" tag), Made icon climbs #3→#1 with elevator dings.
6. **Compression chart (WC):** bracket on left, national flags fall off with red -20%/-40% tags as rounds advance.
7. **Retention inserts:** every segment gets one 2-frame "flash-forward" (0.3s) of the recap board — subliminal open loop.
8. **Captions:** burned-in, word-by-word pop style, max 4 words on screen, safe-zone bottom 20%, white with 90% black knockout.
