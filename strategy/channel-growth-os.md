# Sports Card Investing — YouTube Growth OS

The operating system for building the #1 sports card investing channel. Every video, short, and post ships through this system.

## Mission

Help viewers buy the right boxes **before** everyone else. Avoid overpriced products. Identify undervalued wax. Teach investing principles. Explain risk honestly. Show expected upside with evidence, never hype.

**The one question every video answers:** *"What sports card boxes should I buy today that could be worth significantly more in the future?"*

Warren Buffett's discipline + Gary Vee's urgency + MrBeast's retention engineering + Graham Stephan's numbers-on-screen finance style + Sports Card Investor's hobby fluency. Strong opinions, always backed by research. Never generic. A deliberate **AVOID call in every buy video** — trust is the moat.

## Audience

Collectors · investors · flippers · sports bettors diversifying · parents buying for kids · passive-appreciation seekers. Budgets $50–$10,000 — every video serves every tier via the Budget Board.

## Non-negotiables per video

1. **Research first (Final Rule):** identify the strongest boxes from current data (undervaluation, rookie class, scarcity, historical appreciation, liquidity, long-term potential) before a single script line. Research doc lives in `/research/`.
2. **Every product covered gets:** current retail + eBay solds + historical pricing + pop reports where relevant + rookie/auto checklists + chase cards + print run/odds if known + supply & demand analysis + catalysts + HOF potential + injury/performance trends + historical comp + risk level + expected appreciation + a BUY / WAIT / AVOID call.
3. **Every box gets scored:** Investment Score (1–10), Rarity, Liquidity, Upside, Downside, Risk, Long-Term Hold, Short-Term Flip, Overall Grade.
4. **Budget Board in every buy video:** Best Under $100 / $250 / $500 / $1,000 · Best Overall · Sleeper · High-Risk · Safest.
5. **Retention beat every 20–30 seconds:** price reveal, chart, open loop, comparison, teaser, question, or surprise. No slow sections — cut them.
6. **Faceless by design:** every scene specifies visuals, B-roll, motion graphics, text overlays, SFX, and music so no on-camera talent is ever required.
7. **Compliance line in every description:** *"Not financial advice. Sealed wax is speculative and can lose value. Prices verified on [date]."* Honest risk disclosure is a differentiator, not a disclaimer.

## Script skeleton

**HOOK (0–15s):** pattern interrupt / shock stat / question / promise.
**INTRO (15–60s):** why now, who it's for, preview the biggest surprise.
**BODY:** per product — overview, current price, historical price, why rising, why it may fail, chases, biggest rookie, future value estimate, grade, who should/shouldn't buy.
**ENDING:** recap board → best overall → "What box are YOU buying?" → subscribe for weekly investment updates.

**Voiceover rules:** short sentences. Conversational. No robotic phrasing, no filler. Every sentence teaches, entertains, builds suspense, or raises curiosity — or it gets cut.
