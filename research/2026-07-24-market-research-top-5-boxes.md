### 🚫 THE AVOID — 2025 Panini Prizm Football Hobby at $999.95 — **WAIT**

**The contrarian segment that builds trust. Everyone is screaming "last Prizm Football ever!" We're passing at this price. Here's the evidence:**

1. **Price already reflects the narrative:** $999.95 current, up 20–50% since the license-death news cycle. You are buying the headline, late.
2. **The rookie class didn't cooperate:** preseason OROY favorites Ashton Jeanty and Cam Ward **did not win** — Tetairoa McMillan did (70 rec, 1,014 yds, 7 TD; 41/50 first-place votes). A WR-led class with unproven QBs has historically capped wax ceilings.
3. **Supply is worse than the story:** hobby reporting indicates Panini **increased late-2025 production** anticipating exactly this last-chance demand. "Last ever" + "printed extra" = a scarcity story with an asterisk.
4. **Better expression of the same thesis:** the $609.95 International box, or simply waiting — end-of-license products historically dip once FOMO exhausts, before the true long-term climb.

**Recommendation: WAIT for sub-$800, or buy International. Not a never — a not-at-this-price.**
